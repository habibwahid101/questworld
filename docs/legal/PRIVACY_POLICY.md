1. Introduction

Questra World LLC (“Questra World”, “QW”, “Company”, “we”, “us” or “our”) respects the privacy of individuals who use its website, membership platform and related services.

This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, how we use and protect it, when we may share it and the choices available to individuals.

This policy applies to visitors, applicants, registered Members, referral participants and individuals who communicate with Questra World.

Questra World is formed in Delaware, United States, and intends to provide Services internationally. Additional privacy rights or requirements may apply depending on where an individual lives.

2. Information we collect

Depending on how you use the Services, we may collect the following categories of information.

2.1 Identity and registration information

Name and other registration details.

Email address.

Telephone number.

Account username and Member identifier.

Referral or sponsor identifier.

Country or location information supplied during registration.

Identity-verification information or documents, where required.

2.2 Account and security information

Account creation and update records.

Login and authentication records.

Password-reset and account-recovery events.

Security and verification events.

Records of consent and acceptance of Terms, Privacy Policy and other applicable documents.

Information used to investigate suspected unauthorised access or abuse.

We do not need to store a Member's plain-text password to operate the account. Authentication credentials are handled through the platform's authentication mechanisms.

2.3 Investment and financial activity

Investment principal and investment identifier.

Investment creation and activation dates.

Funding verification and administrative review records.

Scheduled payment and posted payment history.

Referral commission records.

Withdrawal amounts, status and review history.

Payout network, wallet destination and transaction reference, where supplied or recorded.

Relevant account and transaction history.

2.4 Technical and usage information

When you access the website or platform, we or our service providers may process technical information such as:

IP address and approximate location derived from it.

Browser, device and operating-system information.

Access dates and times.

Pages and platform features accessed.

Error, diagnostic and security logs.

Session and authentication information.

The exact information collected may depend on the browser, device and platform features used.

2.5 Communications

We may collect information you provide when contacting support, submitting a complaint, responding to a verification request or otherwise communicating with us.

3. How we use information

We use personal information for purposes including:

Creating and maintaining Member accounts.

Authenticating Members and protecting account access.

Recording acceptance of legal documents and their versions.

Creating and managing investments.

Recording funding verification and investment activation.

Calculating and recording scheduled monthly payments.

Calculating and recording eligible referral commissions.

Processing and reviewing withdrawal requests.

Recording manually completed USDT payouts and transaction references.

Providing account history and customer support.

Detecting fraud, abuse, security incidents and unauthorised activity.

Maintaining platform performance and reliability.

Meeting applicable legal, regulatory, accounting and recordkeeping obligations.

Communicating important service information and policy updates.

Establishing, exercising or defending legal claims.

We do not use account passwords as ordinary customer-service information and will not ask Members to disclose their passwords to support personnel.

4. Legal grounds for processing

Where applicable privacy law requires a legal basis for processing, we may rely on one or more of the following, depending on the purpose and jurisdiction:

Contract: processing necessary to create an account, provide requested Services or administer an investment or withdrawal.

Legal obligation: processing required for applicable legal, regulatory, accounting or recordkeeping duties.

Legitimate interests: processing necessary to secure the platform, prevent fraud, maintain records, improve services or protect the Company and Members, where those interests are not overridden by individual rights.

Consent: processing based on consent where consent is legally required, including certain optional communications or technologies.

Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw that consent as permitted by applicable law. Withdrawal does not make earlier lawful processing invalid.

5. How we share information

We may share personal information where reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including with:

5.1 Technology and infrastructure providers

We may use service providers to host, operate, secure and maintain the platform, authentication systems, databases, communications and technical infrastructure. These providers may process information on our behalf under applicable contractual and security arrangements.

5.2 Payment, wallet and transaction services

Where a Member requests a payment or withdrawal, relevant transaction information may be shared with the external wallet, blockchain network, payment service or other provider involved in that transaction.

Blockchain transactions may be publicly visible and may be difficult or impossible to reverse. A public blockchain address or transaction hash may be associated with a Member's activity.

5.3 Professional advisers and service providers

We may share relevant information with lawyers, accountants, auditors, insurers, consultants and other professional advisers where necessary for their services.

5.4 Legal, regulatory and safety disclosures

We may disclose information where we reasonably believe disclosure is required by law, court order, lawful governmental request, regulatory obligation or necessary to protect the rights, safety, property or security of Questra World, Members or others.

5.5 Business transfers

If Questra World undergoes a merger, restructuring, financing, acquisition or sale of assets, information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to applicable law and appropriate safeguards.

We do not sell personal information to third parties for their independent marketing purposes.

6. International data transfers

Questra World is formed in the United States and intends to provide Services internationally. Personal information may therefore be processed in the United States and other countries where the Company or its service providers operate.

Those countries may have privacy laws different from the laws where you live.

Where applicable law requires safeguards for international transfers, the Company will use an appropriate transfer mechanism or safeguard required by that law.

7. Data retention

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including:

Maintaining Member accounts.

Administering investments and payment histories.

Recording commissions and withdrawals.

Preserving document acceptance and transaction audit records.

Meeting legal, tax, accounting, security and regulatory obligations.

Resolving complaints and disputes.

Establishing, exercising or defending legal claims.

Retention periods may differ depending on the type of information and applicable legal requirements.

When information is no longer required, we will take reasonable steps to delete it, anonymise it or otherwise securely dispose of it, subject to legal and operational requirements.

8. Information security

Questra World uses technical and organisational measures intended to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, alteration and disclosure.

These measures may include access controls, authentication, restricted administrative permissions, security monitoring, backups and service-provider safeguards.

No website, database, network or electronic transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Members should protect their credentials, use secure devices and notify the Company promptly if they suspect unauthorised account access.

9. Cookies and similar technologies

The website or platform may use cookies, session technologies or similar mechanisms necessary to operate the website, maintain sessions, support authentication, protect security or remember preferences.

If analytics or optional tracking technologies are used, the Company will provide additional information and obtain consent where required by applicable law.

You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Disabling essential cookies may affect some platform features.

10. Your privacy rights

Depending on your country or state of residence, you may have rights to:

Request access to personal information held about you.

Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.

Request deletion of personal information, subject to applicable exceptions.

Request a copy of certain information in a portable format.

Object to or request restriction of certain processing.

Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

Complain to an applicable data-protection authority.

Exercise other rights provided by local privacy law.

To make a request, contact us using the details in Section 15.

We may need to verify your identity before responding. Some information may need to be retained where required by law, necessary for transaction records or needed to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

11. Children's privacy

The Services are not intended for individuals under 18 or below the age of legal majority in their jurisdiction, whichever is higher.

We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children for membership or investment participation. If we learn that an ineligible child has provided personal information, we will take appropriate steps consistent with applicable law.

12. Third-party websites and services

The platform may contain links to external websites, wallet services, blockchain explorers or other third-party services.

Those services operate under their own privacy policies and terms. Questra World does not control the privacy practices of independent third parties. Members should review the relevant policies before using those services.

13. Automated processing and account decisions

The platform uses automated systems to support account authentication, scheduled payment-cycle checks, balance calculations, transaction recording and security controls.

Administrative decisions, including funding verification and withdrawal review, may involve authorised human administrators.

Where applicable law gives you rights concerning decisions made solely by automated processing, you may contact us to ask about those rights.
