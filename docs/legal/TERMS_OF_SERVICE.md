5. Monthly payment schedule

The QW investment payment model uses twelve scheduled monthly payment cycles.

Under the current approved calculation:

The scheduled total across twelve payment cycles is equal to two times the original investment principal.

The scheduled amount for each cycle is calculated as two times the original principal divided by twelve.

The first payment becomes due 30 days after investment activation.

Each subsequent payment is scheduled at 30-day intervals after the preceding due date.

There is no daily payment entitlement.

For example, an investment with a principal of 1,000 units has twelve scheduled payments of approximately 166.67 units each, subject to the platform's rounding and final-cycle calculation. The aggregate scheduled amount across all twelve cycles is 2,000 units.

The platform records each payment cycle separately. A payment is reflected in the Member's account history when it has been posted by the platform.

The daily scheduled system process is an administrative mechanism for checking whether an individual investment has reached its next 30-day due date. It does not create a daily return entitlement.

If a scheduled processing run is delayed or interrupted, the platform may process eligible outstanding monthly cycles in a subsequent run, subject to the investment's remaining payment cycles and applicable system controls. This does not change the underlying 30-day payment schedule.

The Member should rely on the payment records displayed in their account and should report any apparent discrepancy to the Company.
